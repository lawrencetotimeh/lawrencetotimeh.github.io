Move 4 · Protest field guide

Know what we're marching for

1. Write the letters. 2. Know their stance. 3. Recalibrate if needed. 4. Take it to the streets.

When they answer, here's your next move

Yes
Hold them to it. Ask them to write the Justice Department and the Park Service, and to send you a copy.
Form letter
Write back: "That's not an answer. Yes or no?" Call the office.
No
Post it. Then ask them in person at their next town hall, on camera.
Silent
No answer in 21 days? Now you march, with a destination: their district office.

How to march with a purpose

  • Destination: the official's district office. Look it up on their website.
  • Bring: printed copies of every letter your group sent.
  • One demand: "Where do you stand? Put it in writing."
  • Film it: the handoff, the staff, the answer. Post it with the hashtag.
  • Stay peaceful. Don't block doors or traffic. Follow police orders and ask for a supervisor if something's wrong.

We don't destroy things because they're there. We replace what doesn't work for us with something that does. That includes public servants, at the ballot box.
