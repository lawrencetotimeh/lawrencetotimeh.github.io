STEP 4The video formula

Every video has one job: make people keep watching. Lawrence and Deon each have their own take. Take what works from both.

1. The hookFirst 3 seconds. Say what everybody thinks is true, or ask what everybody wonders.
2. The proofThe middle. A fact, a number, a clip, or someone you talked to.
3. The twistRight before the end. The part nobody saw coming.
4. The call to actionThe last line. Comment, share, follow, or show up.
See an example
  1. Hook: "Everybody says you need an expensive camera to start a channel."
  2. Proof: "I filmed this whole video on my phone."
  3. Twist: "The camera was never the problem. Most people never post because they're scared of what people will think."
  4. Call to action: "Comment the first video you'd make."
