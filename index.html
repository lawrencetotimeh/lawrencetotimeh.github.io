About

Lawrence Totimeh Jr., also known as Agent Kodak, has lived from the DMV to California to Africa and back to the DMV. A former Sergeant in the Army National Guard, he deployed to Iraq in support of Operation New Dawn, and has spent more than ten years in education, workforce development, and community leadership across the DMV.

He holds a Master of Science in Career & Technical Education, with a concentration in Project Management, from Virginia State University, where he also completed his undergrad.

Liberia Forward is the nonprofit Lawrence founded and leads in Liberia. It addresses food insecurity and builds economic empowerment by growing and selling cassava.

Lawrence speaks and runs workshops for schools and community groups. Topics include teaching students to use a physical planner and sync a digital calendar on their phone, showing parents how to link calendars so their kids stay organized, and teenage decision-making.
