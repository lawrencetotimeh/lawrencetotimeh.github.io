Speaking & Workshops

Civic education that leaves people with something to do

Lawrence is an educator, not an attorney. He teaches how the system is built, in plain language, from the public record, so audiences leave knowing the rules and the next move. Every talk ends with a documentation exercise, not a Q&A.

Documentation Beats Conversation

Records, receipts, and accountability.

You leave with: how a paper trail wins, and a template to start building one in your community.

Know the Rules

Probable cause, grand juries, civil suits, and your rights at each step.

You leave with: a plain-language map of each step and a one-page "your rights at each step" card.

Who Holds the Levers

How the justice system is structured.

You leave with: a filled-in chart of who decides what in your county, and where pressure actually lands.

From the Front Line

Civic power from Fannie Lou Hamer to today. Available with a nonpartisan voter education workshop built on official state resources.

You leave with: the civic power timeline and a voter-ready checklist.

About

Lawrence Totimeh Jr., also known as Agent Kodak, teaches how systems work, especially the justice system, and organizes communities to change them. A former Sergeant in the Army National Guard, he served in Baghdad, Iraq under Operation New Dawn, and has spent more than ten years in education, coaching, and community leadership from the DMV to Los Angeles to Liberia.

He holds a Master of Science in Career & Technical Education, with a concentration in Project Management, from Virginia State University, where he also completed his undergrad.

Liberia Forward is the organization Lawrence founded and leads in Liberia: agriculture, infrastructure, and civic education in his parents' homeland.

On YouTube he teaches the law to the public through the School of Law lessons and The Community. He is an educator, not an attorney.
