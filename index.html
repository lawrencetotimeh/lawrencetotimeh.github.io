About

Lawrence Totimeh Jr., also known as Agent Kodak, is an Army combat veteran and an educator of more than ten years. He founded Liberia Forward, works as a project manager, and stays active as a community leader across the DMV.

He holds a master's in Career and Technical Studies, with a concentration in Project Management, from Virginia State University, where he also did his undergrad.

Liberia Forward is his nonprofit. It addresses food insecurity and builds economic empowerment in Liberia by growing and selling cassava.
