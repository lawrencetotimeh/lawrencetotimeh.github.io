The Community · theory to practice

Our enemy
is injustice.
Not people.

We learn the theory. Then we go into our communities and practice it. The Community is what the lessons become after Lesson 6. Members are Agents of Change: people who understand the system, teach their neighbors, and take action.

How to join

Follow @AgentKodak on YouTube. Comment "reporting for duty" on any lesson. That's it. No dues, no forms.

Report for duty on YouTube ↗ Start with Lesson 1

Lawrence is an educator, not an attorney. The Community teaches the public record and how the system works. It does not give legal advice.

Theory to practice

  • Learn the law. One real case, one lesson at a time.
  • Document. Records, receipts, the paper trail.
  • Act. Vote, follow up, teach others.

Agent rules

  • Learn the law
  • Document everything
  • Vote
  • Follow up
  • Never harass. Our enemy is injustice, not people.

The creed

I will always place the mission first. I will never accept defeat. I will never quit. I will always bring the documentation.
